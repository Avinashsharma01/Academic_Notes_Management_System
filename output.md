### IMPORTANT PROJECT REPORT GUIDELINES

- **Typing & Format:** The B.Tech. project report shall be computer
  typed (English, Font - Times New Roman, Size-12 point) and printed on
  A4 size paper.

- **Binding:** The B.Tech. project report shall be hard bound with cover
  page in black colour. For Major Project, hard binding is compulsory
  (do not use spiral binding).

- **Cover Printing:** The name of the candidate, degree (specifying the
  specialization), year of submission, name of the University including
  college name shall be printed in black on the outer cover.

- **Margins & Spacing:** The report shall be typed on one side only with
  double space with a margin of 3.5 cm on the left, 2.5 cm on the top,
  and 1.25 cm on the right and at the bottom.

- **Sequence Flow:** Title page (inner cover) should be given first,
  then the Certificate by the candidate and supervisor(s), followed by
  an Abstract (not exceeding 1500 words),

> Acknowledgment, List of Figures/Tables, and Content Index with page
> numbers.

- **References Style:** References must be indicated in the body text
  giving author name and year of publication (e.g., Singh and Shan,
  2002). At the end of the report, references should be listed in
  alphabetical order specifying authors\' initials, journal/book title,
  volume number, pages, and year of publication.
